# Developer Certificate of Origin

Contributions use Developer Certificate of Origin version 1.1. By signing off
a commit, a contributor certifies that they created the contribution or have
the right to submit it under this repository's open-source license, understand
that the contribution and sign-off are public, and agree that the project may
distribute it under the project license.

The canonical text is available at <https://developercertificate.org/>. Add a
sign-off with `git commit -s`. No contributor license agreement is required.
